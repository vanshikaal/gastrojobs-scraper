# How the reel maps to the "Excellent" rubric column

Reel: *Geet Needed Life Prototyping*, 44 s, 1080×1920. Timestamps refer to `out/geet-needed-life-prototyping.mp4`.

## 1. Understanding of Life Design
- **Prototyping is defined accurately**, in Designing Your Life terms, at 17–26s. The narration: "exploring a possible future through small experiments, so you can test assumptions and learn before committing." The destination is labelled *a possible future*, not "the" future.
- **Both prototype types are named and shown**, at 26–34s:
  1. a **prototype conversation**: an honest talk about their future
  2. a **prototype experience**: a low-stakes weekend together, explicitly *no wedding*
- **Iteration and reassessment** at the end of that section. A clipboard moves from *I assumed* (crossed out) to *I learned* to *plan v2*, and gets a REASSESSED stamp. The stepping stones read TEST → LEARN → REASSESS.
- **Limits are stated**: "(no crystal ball. just better info.)" makes clear that prototyping reduces guesswork, not risk to zero.

## 2. Application & Interpretation
- **Independent interpretation of a real story**: Geet's famous runaway plan is reframed as an untested assumption. "Anshuman was a hypothesis, not a plan." Their relationship is acknowledged; the gap is *testing*, not talking.
- **Real-life connection**, at 41–44s: "So... what's your Anshuman?" Hypothesis cards read *new job? / new city? / big course?*, turning the film into a prompt about the viewer's own untested assumptions.
- **Personal voice**: "I think we can agree, girl desperately needed a Life Design Lab lecture."

## 3. Creativity & Storytelling
| Device | Where |
|---|---|
| Humour | ATTENDANCE COMPULSORY stamp; "approved by: Geet / reviewed by: Geet / evidence: vibes"; *cricket noises* |
| Characters & role-play | Geet vs **LDL Geet** from an alternate "LDL universe" |
| Visual metaphors | a hypothesis index card for Anshuman; one giant leap vs stepping stones |
| Contrasting perspectives | COMMIT vs SMALL STEPS split screen; dramatic Geet vs calm LDL Geet |
| Unexpected twist | an over-the-top Bollywood plot twist, cut off by a record scratch and a tiny checklist |

## 4. Communication & Audience Engagement
- **Hook** (0–7s): a well-loved film character, a mid-leap freeze frame, then a cheeky lecture.
- **Coherent arc**: problem → punchline → concept → application → twist → takeaway.
- **Memorable takeaway**: TEST. LEARN. THEN DECIDE., followed by the viewer-facing question.
- **Accessibility**: the movie context is explained on screen; there's one idea per scene, short captions and labelled visuals.

## 5. Storytelling Presentation & Expression
- **Sound used for timing**: silence lets each joke breathe, *cricket noises* fill the NOT VERIFIED freeze, and a record scratch cuts off the Bollywood melodrama.
- **SFX on every key action**: stamp thumps, whooshes on transitions, pops on reveals, wedding bells, crickets.
- **Subtitles**: burned in, synced to the narration, with key terms highlighted, kept inside Instagram's safe zone (`subtitles.srt` is included).
- **Pacing & transitions**: freeze frames and comedic pauses, snap-zooms (DUN. DUN. DUNNN.), slide and portal transitions, squash-and-stretch animation.
